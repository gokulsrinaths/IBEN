# Owner decisions before live submissions

- Confirm the responsible operator and verified leadership information for publication.
- Provide monitored contact addresses and decide who handles applications, nominations and enquiries.
- Confirm the 2026 intake status, eligibility details, selection timetable and any fees. No dates or fees have been assumed.
- Confirm reviewer responsibilities, conflicts handling and how corrections or concerns will be addressed.
- Approve operational privacy/terms, retention and deletion rules, nominee consent and portfolio publication permissions.
- Choose the backend and private file-storage provider, access roles and submission acknowledgement process. Submissions remain unavailable until these are connected and tested.
- Confirm the production domain and canonical origin before deployment.

Recognition records, certificates, additional programmes and membership features are outside this implementation.
